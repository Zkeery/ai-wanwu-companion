第一轮参考：[原图](../../阶段7/R7.14古灵精怪免费体验/apple-9b.jpg)；输出 [apple-atlas.png](apple-atlas.png)。

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the reference apple character into ONE game-animation sprite atlas: EXACTLY FOUR COLUMNS and THREE ROWS, 12 equal rectangular cells in a strict regular grid, canvas 1536 by 864, each cell 384 by 288 (4:3). No gutters, borders, lines, words, numbers, symbols or watermark. Transparent background with genuine alpha if supported; otherwise uniform flat neutral mid-gray #808080 across every cell, with no floor or cast shadow. Preserve the SAME apple identity from the reference in every frame: realistic red/yellow speckled apple skin, same long curved stem, brown/gold eyes, eyebrows, single front tooth, stubby arms and feet, same 3D render style. Remove the four floating sparks. One whole body per cell, centered, consistent scale, enough margin so no extremity touches cell edge. Fixed front three-quarter camera and identical lighting. Row 1 REST: column1 original neutral standing, column2 arms relax and eyelids half shut, column3 eyes completely closed with peacefully relaxed mouth and slightly lowered body, column4 begins reopening eyes and returns toward original standing. Row 2 WALK: column1 left foot forward right foot back with opposite arm swing, column2 passing pose with weight on left foot and right heel raised, column3 right foot forward left foot back with opposite arm swing, column4 passing pose with weight on right foot and left heel raised. Clearly alternating two feet and body weight; not four near-identical expressions, no whole-frame translations. Row 3 OBSERVE: column1 neutral pose, column2 head and pupils turn toward screen right, column3 head and pupils remain toward screen right and right arm points to that side, column4 retracts pointing arm and turns back toward neutral. Do not invent a target or prop; the actual target is supplied by the application scene. Make a polished, legible sprite atlas with precise grid alignment and consistent identity.
```

第二轮参考第一轮输出；输出 [apple-atlas-v2.png](apple-atlas-v2.png)。

```text
Edit this exact animation atlas, preserving the same twelve character identities and twelve poses in exactly the same 4-column 3-row positions. Make ONE correction: reduce every apple figure to 78% of its current size, centered within its own cell, so every cell has AT LEAST 30 pixels of fully transparent space around ALL edges, including above the tallest stem and below feet. No figure or anti-aliased edge may touch any cell boundary or canvas boundary. Clean away all stray colored speckles or halos outside the actual silhouette. Keep genuine transparent alpha background, no shadows, no checkerboard, no background color. Do not change the facial expressions, skin textures, poses, lighting, or 4-column 3-row grid. No text, labels or lines. Landscape 16:9 canvas, preferably 1536x864 pixels. Each equal cell remains 4:3. The result must be a cleanly separated game sprite atlas.
```
